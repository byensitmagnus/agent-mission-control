# Separate post-run learning

This reference governs learning from task outcomes. An explicitly requested
source-backed design or compatibility update is ordinary skill maintenance:
record its source, scope, rollback and relevant engineering checks, and label
behavioral benefit unmeasured. It does not require an imitation research trial
and must not be presented as a verified learned improvement.

Activate outcome-driven learning only after a completed task reveals a reusable success or failure that
could improve another run. Routine completion does not require a learning phase.
Without a suitable evaluation budget, retain the candidate lesson as a note;
do not launch training or silently promote it into a rule.
Record a compact candidate lesson with the observed behavior, evidence, scope and
counterexample risk; strip secrets, customer data and raw transcripts.

Turn that signal into a reproducible case. In a separate development task, pin
the incumbent skill and evaluator, separate development/selection cases from
held-out regression cases, and propose one bounded rule change. Compare current
and proposed behavior in fresh equivalent contexts. Require an actual improvement
and no higher-priority correctness, safety or routing regression. Instruction
word count, confident language and the training case alone are not quality.

Hold out final tests from candidate selection. If their result changes the next
candidate, they have become development data; use a fresh untouched held-out set
for a new claim. Missing replay or held-out evidence leaves the lesson a proposal,
NOT VERIFIED for adoption. Keep the incumbent on ties or regressions.

Stage the candidate diff, source identity, observed results and rollback source
for review. Adoption is a separate authorized action; check that the target still
matches the reviewed base before replacing it. Preserve conflicting local edits.
Never rewrite the active governing skill mid-mission, auto-install globally or
promote a remembered observation into policy. Optional external learning tools
may supply execution, but AMC requires no learner daemon or scheduled job.
