- **AVO:** retain the existing baseline/candidate/diagnosis record, strict improvement
  and repeated-strategy stop. Add the demonstrated preservation guard. No trajectory
  database; no performance or cost improvement is claimed from this iteration.
- **Context Diamond:** bounded fresh packets remain appropriate. Nonce isolation
  was observed; intended custom roles and their permissions were not verified.
  Keep native Codex as owner of sessions, models, tools and permissions.
- **SkillOpt/learning:** existing offline, held-out, staged proposals remain sufficient.
  Treat the observed failure as a development lesson; no learned rule is globally
  adopted and no active subject rewrote its governing rules. Model/host/source
  provenance belongs in comparative evidence; no mandatory observation pool.
- **Upstream:** directly reopened the pinned Microsoft `skillopt/evaluation/gate.py`:
  strict score improvement and ties retaining the incumbent match existing AMC.
  No semantic-density score bonus, trainer/cache, daemon, installation or imported
  framework. Earlier NVIDIA/publication and orchestrator mining remain historical;
  no new implementation claim is made from those reports alone.
- **Review scope:** do not mandate new per-child artifact schemas or a full runtime
  provenance envelope for trivial tasks. Existing packets and native logs suffice
  when actually available. Independent review and final local checks are recorded
  below; no rule-only proposal is labelled behaviorally verified.
