## AMC-ROUTE-001 — direct_default

- **claim_type:** routing_policy
- **claim:** Direct work is the default for tightly coupled work, a strong lead and high coordination cost. Delegation is for decomposable work or a separate specialist context.
- **evidence_origin:** reasoned_inference
- **source_ids:** NATURE-MMI-2026, ANTHROPIC-EFFECTIVE-AGENTS
- **source_supported_fact:** Kim et al. found the single-agent baseline predicted whether coordination helped. Decomposability mattered more than complexity. Artificial splits often hurt. SWE-bench MAS variants slightly degraded on a 20-instance subset per cell.
- **architectural_inference:** AMC uses a cautious direct default. That is not a theorem that sequential work is always best, and it does not by itself validate sequential specialists.
- **does_not_prove:** A universal sequential rule for all coding agents, or that AMC specialists beat a strong single session.
- **adoption:** adopted
- **enforcement_scope:** instruction_only
